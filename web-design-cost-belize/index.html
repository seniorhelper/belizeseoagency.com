How Much Does Web Design Cost in Belize?

Updated October 19, 2026 · By Eye To Ad Media · Prices as published by each Belize studio on the date above

Direct answer: Belize web designers that publish prices list one-time packages from about $600 for a one-page site to about $3,500 for a large e-commerce site. The studios that state a currency charge from BZ$800 for a 5–10 page site to BZ$2,850 for e-commerce (BK Creative), or from US$1,000 (BZ$2,000) for a WordPress site (FEXP Design). Monthly “website rental” plans start at BZ$75 a month. Add a domain (.bz is US$35 a year at BelizeNIC) and ask whether GST of 12.5% is included.

One-time build or monthly plan?

Belize studios sell websites two ways. A one-time build (BK Creative, FEXP, Idealab, Visuals by Glenn Patrick) means you pay up front and then cover hosting, domain and updates yourself or through a care plan. A monthly plan (IBT Belize, from BZ$75 a month) bundles design, hosting, SSL and maintenance into one fee. Over two years, a BZ$75 plan adds up to BZ$1,800, so compare total cost over the time you expect to keep the site, and ask who owns the site and domain if you stop paying.

What pushes the price up

  • Pages. Most published ladders above scale with page count, from one page to 20 or more.
  • Bookings and payments. Resort, tour and dive businesses need a booking engine or payment gateway; Visuals by Glenn Patrick prices a 17-page resort/tours booking site at $2,400, and e-commerce tiers run highest everywhere.
  • Two languages. Selling to visitors and locals often means English and Spanish pages.
  • Content and photos. If you need copywriting or photography, ask whether it is included; most published packages assume you supply them.

Recurring costs to budget

  • Domain: BelizeNIC, the .bz registry, lists .bz, .com.bz, .net.bz and .co.bz at $35.00 a year (BelizeNIC). A .com from an international registrar is another option.
  • Hosting: BK Creative includes the first year; FEXP Design lists hosting separately; IBT Belize bundles it into the monthly plan.
  • GST: the Belize Tax Service states that “if the supply is standard rated, the rate is 12.5%” (Belize Tax Service). Idealab’s prices say they exclude GST; the others do not say, so ask.
  • Currency: the Belize dollar is pegged at a fixed rate of BZ$2 to US$1 (Central Bank of Belize), so a US$1,000 quote is BZ$2,000.

A website is only half the job

A new site does not bring customers on its own. Before you pay for a build, ask how the designer handles page speed, mobile layout, your Google Business Profile, and the words people in Belize and visitors actually type when they search. A cheaper site that gets found can beat an expensive one nobody sees.
